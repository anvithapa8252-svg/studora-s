Homework Guide 💡

How to solve a difficult question

  1. Read the question carefully.
  2. Write down what is given.
  3. Identify what you need to find.
  4. Choose the correct formula or idea.
  5. Solve step by step and check your answer.

Tip: Understanding why an answer works is more useful than memorising it.
